01 · Executive Summary

The Platform the Continent
Has Been Waiting For

There are EdTech platforms across Africa that teach children. There are NGOs that deliver financial literacy. There are CSR programmes that feed families. But there is not a single platform that does all three — rooted in an African philosophical framework, delivered through modern technology, pointed toward a future that Africa imagines for itself.

This platform changes that. By placing Ubuntu at the centre of how children learn — not as a tagline but as the actual pedagogical engine — and by anchoring community care in two concrete CSR commitments (Feed a Mother. Help a Sista.), it creates a category that no competitor can authentically enter.

02 · Competitor Landscape

Who You Are Up Against

The competitive field has two tiers: global NGO incumbents delivering financial education, and African EdTech platforms delivering digital learning. Your platform sits at the intersection of both — and is vulnerable to neither, because neither has built around Ubuntu philosophy or community CSR as core product architecture.

Aflatoun Deep-Dive · Why It Matters

Aflatoun is the strongest named comparator because it combines scale and donor credibility. Their footprint proves demand for social and financial education globally.

⚠️

Where They Break in Africa

The model is curriculum-led and grant-routed, not platform-led. It lacks app + web delivery, low-connectivity product mechanics, and Ubuntu as a first-principles pedagogy.

✅

Your Counter-Position

Build what they cannot: a Pan-African digital product rooted in Ubuntu, multi-language by design, and tied to direct CSR outcomes through Feed a Mother and Help a Sista.

04 · CSR Model

Community Care Built Into the Product

Feed a Mother and Help a Sista are not side programmes. They are embedded CSR rails linked to learning and funding flows, creating measurable and fundable outcomes for partners.

CSR PILLAR 01

Feed a Mother

Link sponsorship and engagement metrics to maternal food support in participating communities. Deliver traceable outputs for CSR partners and direct relief for households.

CSR PILLAR 02

Help a Sista

Route impact funding into women and girls through scholarships, skills support and micro-support pathways tied to measurable participation and outcomes.

🏦

Foundation & CSR Partners

Safaricom Foundation, MTN Foundation, Equity Bank Foundation and aligned donors.

📈

Impact Reporting

Sponsor dashboards designed for auditable impact metrics and ongoing funding confidence.

🧩

Product + Impact Loop

Learning activity strengthens outcomes data; outcomes data unlocks sustainable partner funding.
